# Templates

Minimum artifact templates added under `docs/dev_flow/templates/`:

- `ISSUE.md`
- `MILESTONES.md`
- `BOARD.md`
- `BOARD_DECISION.json`
- `NEXT_GOAL.md`
- `STATE.md`
- `WORKER_RESULT.md`
- `PR_REVIEW.md`
- `DECISIONS.md`
- `EXPLAIN.md`

The templates are intentionally minimal. They define required headings and
decision fields without adding execution automation.
