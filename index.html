Architecture At A Glance

The top-level Tenn story is deliberately smaller than the repo graph. Cockpit is the client layer, FastAPI is the authority boundary, background work runs through Celery, inference flows through llama.cpp, truth lives in Postgres and Qdrant plus local memory surfaces, and the main external inputs are ASX filings and news.

What Matters Operationally

These are the surfaces most people actually look for when trying to understand Tenn.

Chat Runtime Agent loop, SSE streaming, verification-aware prompts, and recent-turn context.
Memory Surfaces Chat history, dossier state, company memory, market memory, and commentary context.
Company Financials Structured financial rows flow from backend truth APIs backed by `asx_periodic_financials`.
Written Evidence Filings, announcement chunks, news, commentary, and qualitative notes remain visible data surfaces.
View Routing `/docs/tenn-system-map.html` is the deeper runtime map, `/docs/architecture/tenn_system_map_frontend.html` explains authority flow, and `/docs/architecture/tenn_ticker_map.html?ticker=BHP` shows one company’s actual data in the map.
